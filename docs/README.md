# OctoAcme Project Management Processes

This README serves as the central entry point for all OctoAcme project management process documentation.

## Overview

OctoAcme follows a structured, iterative project management methodology designed to deliver customer value quickly and reliably. The approach emphasizes clear ownership, data-informed decisions, and continuous improvement across every project phase — from initiation through retrospective.

### Key Principles

- **Customer-first**: Prioritize customer value and usability in every decision
- **Iterative delivery**: Deliver small, testable increments to reduce risk and gather feedback early
- **Clear ownership**: Every project has a named PM and Product Lead who are accountable for outcomes
- **Data-informed**: Measure impact and iterate based on evidence rather than assumptions
- **Psychological safety**: Encourage open feedback, experimentation, and learning from failures

## Quick Reference

### Key Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Project Manager (PM)** | Overall project coordination, risk management, and stakeholder communication |
| **Product Lead (PdM)** | Product vision, prioritization, and customer alignment |
| **Engineering Lead** | Technical direction, architecture decisions, and delivery quality |
| **Designer** | UX/UI design, usability testing, and design system compliance |
| **Stakeholders** | Business sponsors and customers who provide requirements and approvals |

### Lifecycle Phases

1. **Initiation** — Define the problem, goals, and team
2. **Planning** — Break down work, estimate effort, and schedule sprints
3. **Execution** — Build, track progress, and manage risks
4. **Release** — Validate readiness, deploy, and monitor
5. **Retrospective** — Reflect, learn, and improve processes

### Communication Cadence

| Meeting | Frequency | Duration | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standup | Daily | 15 min | Progress, blockers, and dependencies |
| PM + PdM Sync | Weekly | — | Alignment and planning |
| Stakeholder Update | Weekly | — | Status, decisions, and escalations |
| Retrospective | Post-sprint / milestone | — | Learnings and process improvements |

## Getting Started

### New Team Member

1. Read the [Project Management Overview](octoacme-project-management-overview.md) to understand the methodology.
2. Review [Roles and Personas](octoacme-roles-and-personas.md) to understand your responsibilities and those of your teammates.
3. Check the current project's planning document to see the active sprint and backlog.
4. Join the daily standup and introduce yourself to the team.

### New Project Manager

1. Start with the [Project Initiation](octoacme-project-initiation.md) guide to kick off your project correctly.
2. Work through [Project Planning](octoacme-project-planning.md) to build your roadmap and first sprint.
3. Use [Execution and Tracking](octoacme-execution-and-tracking.md) to set up your progress tracking and status reporting.
4. Familiarize yourself with [Risks and Communication](octoacme-risks-and-communication.md) to proactively manage risks and keep stakeholders informed.
5. Bookmark [Release and Deployment](octoacme-release-and-deployment.md) and [Retrospective and Continuous Improvement](octoacme-retrospective-and-continuous-improvement.md) for the end of each milestone.
